# ClueLab demonstration data

Eight deliberately short articles, eight original practice clues (Standard), and
eight guided variants (Easy). This is a
functional fixture, not a representative accuracy benchmark or official Jeopardy
content. The private clue packs and answer keys are in `questions.txt` and
`easy-questions.txt`, outside `corpus/` and outside the served web assets.
Easy adds wording and wordplay hints visible to both participants; it does not
change the search engine, scoring rules, or original benchmark. These are
editorial difficulty labels, not levels calibrated with player testing.

Article summaries were adapted for this project from the repository's existing
`wiki-example.txt` coursework sample (the collection is identified as
`wiki-subset-20140602` in the original repository). Exact Wikipedia revision IDs
were not preserved in that sample. These are historical educational summaries,
not claims to reproduce current articles.
