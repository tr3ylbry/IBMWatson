[[British Standards]]
CATEGORIES: Standards organizations, United Kingdom
British Standards are produced by BSI Group, incorporated under a Royal Charter. Products certified under designated schemes can carry the Kitemark.

[[Building society]]
CATEGORIES: Cooperatives, Financial institutions
A building society is a financial institution owned by its members as a mutual organisation. Building societies offer banking services, especially mortgage lending and savings accounts.

[[Burwash Hall]]
CATEGORIES: University of Toronto buildings, Gothic Revival architecture
Burwash Hall is a residence building at Toronto's Victoria College. Construction began in 1911 and finished in 1913. Architect Henry Sproatt designed its Neo-Gothic turrets, gargoyles and battlements.

[[Body mass index]]
CATEGORIES: Human body weight, Nutrition
Body mass index, abbreviated BMI, relates a person's mass to height. It is calculated by dividing mass in kilograms by the square of height in metres.

[[Behistun Inscription]]
CATEGORIES: Persian history, Inscriptions
The Behistun Inscription records the story of Darius the Great. Its parallel versions in Old Persian, Elamite and Babylonian helped scholars decipher cuneiform, much as the Rosetta Stone helped with Egyptian hieroglyphs.

[[Barry Goldwater]]
CATEGORIES: United States Senators from Arizona
Barry Goldwater served five terms as a United States Senator from Arizona. The Republican Party nominated him for president in 1964. He was known as Mr. Conservative.

[[Bob Young (businessman)]]
CATEGORIES: Open source people, Red Hat people
Bob Young was an entrepreneur associated with the open source software company Red Hat. He founded the self-publishing website Lulu.com in 2002 and purchased the Hamilton Tiger-Cats football team in 2003.

[[Blue Steel (missile)]]
CATEGORIES: British aviation history
The Avro Blue Steel was a British air-launched nuclear stand-off missile built to arm the V bomber force. It preceded the Royal Navy's Polaris submarine deterrent.
