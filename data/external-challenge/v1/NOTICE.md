# External Research Data Notice

Local benchmark adaptation of **TriviaQA unfiltered 1.0**, Mandar Joshi, Eunsol
Choi, Daniel S. Weld and Luke Zettlemoyer, ACL 2017:
[paper](https://aclanthology.org/P17-1147/),
[official dataset](https://nlp.cs.washington.edu/triviaqa/),
[official repository](https://github.com/mandarjoshi90/triviaqa).

The official repository states that Apache-2.0 applies to its code and data;
`LICENSE-TriviaQA.txt` preserves that license. **The downloaded archive's README
also restricts the dataset and derived data to non-commercial research**, and
disclaims UW ownership of underlying question/document copyright. Its exact notice
is preserved in `UPSTREAM-README.txt`. We do not resolve these overlapping terms
into a blanket commercial or public redistribution permission. Public/commercial
rights review remains open. Do not use this material as generated playable content.

Questions originated with external trivia enthusiasts/sites, not this assistant.
Individual author names/revision IDs are not provided; each selected record keeps
the upstream QuestionId and QuestionSource, without fabricating authors or dates.
No trivia-site scraping, authentication bypass or live evidence crawling is used.

Changes: deterministic subset/split selection, unsupported/media/duplicate
exclusions, mechanical projection of supplied value/aliases/entity title into
article-title labels, existing ClueLab normalization and separate title coverage.
**Question wording is verbatim.** No manually invented question or alias is added.
Question/answer selections, full upstream files and detailed answer-bearing
reports are local ignored research artifacts, never game assets or indexed text.
Committed identities/fingerprints, selection/exclusion accounting and aggregate
software measurements are not a public content approval. Preserve notices when
reproducing the local workflow.

Searchable text is separately attributed to Wikipedia contributors under the
historical export's CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution. It is a processed 2014-named subset,
not current/all Wikipedia; exact page revision IDs and processing provenance are
unknown. Filename dates do not establish immutable revisions. Full articles are
kept locally, not redistributed here; embedded third-party content may retain
separate rights. No human review, playtest or difficulty calibration is claimed.
